CometOTP 8.0.0 is a complete redesign.

New: a Security screen for PIN, password and fingerprint unlock; a Tags screen with colours and counts; Support & FAQs; a copy button on every card; and a choice of app icon.

Improved: Material 3 Expressive throughout, a new typeface and launcher icon, a docked bottom bar, and clearer cards in dark mode.

Fixed: crashes after visiting Settings, a restore that could hang, unreadable status bar icons and invisible dialog buttons.
